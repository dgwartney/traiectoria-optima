# Passenger Name Records

# Definition

PNR stands for *Passenger Name Record*.

It is a unique six-character code (letters and numbers) linked to a digital file in an airline database
that stores your travel itinerary, seat choices, and personal details. Also called a booking reference
or record locator, it is required to check in or change your flight.

## What a PNR Contains

- Passenger full name
- Flight numbers
- Dates and times
- Departure and arrival airports
- Seat assignments
- Payment and contact info

## Why You Need It

- To do online check-in
- To choose or change seats
- To add bags or special requests
- To track flight updates or status


The International Air Transport Association (IATA), the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO),
and Airlines for America (ATA) jointly developed the industry standards to ensure different computer
reservation systems can talk to each other. While it is an industry-wide standard rather than a mandatory law,
five specific elements are strictly required to create a valid booking.

The 5 Mandatory Elements (PRINT):
- Passenger name
- Received-from (identifying who booked the flight)
- Itinerary (flight segments and dates)
- Number for contact (phone or email)
- Ticketing details (time limits or ticket status)
